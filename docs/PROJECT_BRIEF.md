# Project Brief: GTA 6 Launch Clipper

## Who I am

I'm a solo developer entering a 30-day hackathon. I want to build a small product that people actually pay for, launch it fast, and make as much profit as possible within the hackathon window.

## The hackathon

- **Name:** The $100K Hackathon (run by Ship or Die, sponsored by Higgsfield)
- **Starts:** Monday, November 16, 2026. My copy of the rules says 6:00 PM local time, but the official page may say 3:00 PM. Assume the earlier time.
- **Ends:** Exactly 30 days later, Wednesday, December 16, 2026, at the same time.
- **Prize:** $100,000 for the most profit. 2nd and 3rd place get Apple products.

### The rules that matter

1. **Brand new product only.** Nothing built, launched, sold or registered before the start. No domain, no store listing, no TrustMRR listing before launch day, or I get disqualified.
2. **Must be software or a mobile app.** No services, agencies, consulting, courses or physical goods. The product must work by itself, not depend on me doing work for customers by hand.
3. **Solo only.** No co-founders, no team.
4. **Ranked by profit, not revenue.** Profit = money received minus refunds, chargebacks, payment fees and any money spent on ads or promotion.
5. **No paid ads, ideally.** Any money spent on ads, sponsored posts, influencers or affiliate payouts comes off my profit and must be tracked with receipts.
6. **Not counted against profit:** hosting, software, AI and API costs. So it's fine to spend on good tools.
7. **Payments must go through Polar** (already set up), connected to TrustMRR for verification. Keep it connected the whole time.
8. **No cheating.** No buying my own product, no friends or family buying, no asking my audience to buy "to help me win." Every customer must be real and traceable.
9. **Annual or upfront payments count in full,** so one-time passes and upfront plans are better than monthly subscriptions.
10. **Not allowed:** adult content, gambling, deepfakes of real people, anything illegal.

## What we're building

**A clipping tool made specifically for GTA 6 content.**

GTA 6 launches on **November 19, 2026**, three days after the hackathon starts. Thousands of people will start streaming it and trying to grow on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels during launch month.

Our product: a streamer or clipper gives us their stream, and we give back ready-to-post short vertical videos of the best GTA moments, with captions and a catchy title. No manual editing needed.

### Why it can win

- Perfect timing: the biggest game launch in years happens inside our 30 days.
- Clearly software, so it fits the rules.
- It focuses only on GTA moments (chases, crashes, heists, funny moments), unlike general clipping tools.
- We can find customers for free by reaching out directly to people streaming GTA 6.

### Who we sell to

1. **Small and mid-size GTA 6 streamers** who want to grow on short-form platforms but don't have time to edit.
2. **Clippers:** people who get paid per view to post clips of streamers and need a lot of clips fast.

We'll test both groups and focus on whichever one actually pays.

### How we charge

- A one-time **"Launch Pass"** instead of a monthly subscription. Example: $29 for 30 days, or $49 for the launch season.
- Set a fair usage limit (for example, a maximum number of stream hours) so heavy users don't cost more than they pay.
- Offer a short refund guarantee to make buying easier, keeping in mind refunds reduce profit.

### How we get customers (no paid ads)

- Find people streaming GTA 6, clip a few moments from their stream for free, and send them the clips with an offer to buy more.
- Post the best clips (with permission) on our own TikTok and Shorts accounts.
- Share in GTA and streamer communities on Reddit and Discord.

## Things to avoid

- Don't use Rockstar or GTA logos, artwork or branded text in the product or marketing. Describe it as a tool for GTA 6 content, not an official product.
- Don't download other people's YouTube videos without permission. Only work with streams the customer owns and connects themselves, or files they upload.
- Don't register anything for the product before the hackathon starts.

## The plan

- **Days 1 to 3 (before GTA launches):** build a simple first version that turns one stream into a few good clips, plus a working Polar checkout.
- **Days 3 to 7:** message 30 to 50 streamers and clippers every day with free sample clips. Start selling.
- **Day 7 checkpoint:**
  - 5 or more paying customers: go all-in.
  - Interest but no payments: change the price or the target buyer.
  - No interest: drop the idea and pivot.
- **Days 7 to 30:** improve clip quality based on feedback, keep doing outreach daily, and push launch passes while GTA hype is at its peak.

## What success looks like

- Real, paying customers within the first week.
- As much verified profit as possible by December 16, 2026.
- A product people would keep using after the hackathon, ideally expanding to other games later.

## How I want you to help

- Keep everything simple and fast to ship. Speed beats perfection in this hackathon.
- Always check that what we build follows the hackathon rules above.
- Point out anything that could get us disqualified.
- Prioritize features that help us get paying customers sooner.
